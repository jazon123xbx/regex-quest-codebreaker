HOW TO PLAY

  1. Read the target regex pattern displayed on the card.
  2. Enter a string that matches the complete pattern.
  3. Press Decode or hit Enter to submit.
  4. Correct answers earn up to 10 points.
  5. Each elapsed second removes 1 point from your reward.
  6. Incorrect answers can be retried with no penalty.
  7. Skip awards 0 points and reveals the explanation.
  8. Crack all 10 vaults — including the Boss — to finish the mission.

REGEX FIELD GUIDE

\dDigit (0-9)
\wWord character (a-z, A-Z, 0-9, _)
[A-Z]Uppercase A through Z
[a-z]Lowercase a through z
[0-9]Digit range 0 through 9
.Any single character
+One or more of the preceding
*Zero or more of the preceding
?Optional (zero or one)
{3}Exactly three of the preceding
{2,5}Two through five of the preceding
^Start of string
$End of string
|OR — alternation
(...)Group / capture
[abc]One character from the set
